What is Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement Learning (RL) is a machine learning technique where an intelligent agent learns by interacting with an environment. Instead of being given the correct answer, the agent performs actions and receives rewards or penalties. Over time, it improves its decision-making to maximize the total reward and discover the optimal strategy.

"Learning by Trial and Error"

Advantages Disadvantages
Auto-Learning: The model learns by itself without needing human data. Massive Data: It needs millions of trials to learn a task.
Handles Change: It adapts easily to unpredictable and dynamic environments. Hard Rewards: Designing the scoring system is very difficult.
Constant Growth: The AI automatically improves its strategy over time. Slow Training: Finding the right solution takes a long time.
Game Expert: It excels at complex, multi-step tasks like gaming. Heavy Compute: Running simulations requires powerful and expensive computers.
