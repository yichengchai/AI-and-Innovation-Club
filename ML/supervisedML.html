House Price Prediction

Overview

This project predicts the selling price of a house based on its features. The machine learning model learns from historical housing data and estimates the price of new properties.

Problem Solved

Buying or selling a house can be difficult because property prices vary depending on many complex factors. This project helps to estimate a fair market price automatically, removing human bias.

How it works

  • 1

    Collect historical housing data.

  • 2

    Train a Linear Regression model.

  • 3

    Input new house specifications.

  • 4

    The model predicts the optimal selling price.

Technologies Used

Python pandas numpy scikit-learn Linear Regression

Advantages Disadvantages
High Accuracy: Predictions are precise because the model trains on verified, correct answers. Expensive Labeling: Gathering and tagging thousands of data points requires massive human labor.
Clear Metrics: Success is easy to measure using straightforward math like accuracy scores. Training Errors: Mistakes or bias in the original dataset will corrupt the final model.
Pre-defined Classes: Results are highly predictable since you decide the output categories beforehand. New Case Failure: The AI cannot handle or categorize completely new data types it has never seen.
Boundary Control: Human experts fully control the rules and target guidelines the AI learns. High Computation: Training the model initially demands significant time and computer processing power.
