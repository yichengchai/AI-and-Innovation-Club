Unsupervised Learning

Example ML Projects: Customer Segmentation

Description

Customer Segmentation is an unsupervised learning project that automatically groups customers based on shared behaviors, purchasing habits, or demographics. Unlike supervised learning, this model works without pre-existing labels or answers, discovering hidden patterns in raw business data to help companies target different audiences effectively.

How does it function?

  • 1
    Data Ingestion:

    Gather raw customer metrics such as total spending, shopping frequency, and age.

  • 2
    Feature Scaling:

    Normalize data sizes so large numbers (like salary) do not overwhelm small numbers (like age).

  • 3
    K-Means Clustering:

    The algorithm calculates mathematical distances to find natural group clusters automatically.

  • 4
    Profile Generation:

    Output distinct buyer groups like "High Spenders," "Bargain Hunters," or "Churn Risks."

Technologies Used

Python pandas scikit-learn K-Means Clustering matplotlib

Advantages Disadvantages
No Labeling Needed: Saves massive time and money since data does not require manual tagging. Hard to Validate: Measuring accuracy is difficult because there are no correct answers to check against.
Hidden Pattern Discovery: Finds unexpected insights and groupings that humans might completely miss. Unpredictable Outputs: The AI may group data in ways that make no practical or logical sense to humans.
Raw Data Ready: Works directly on raw, messy, and unstructured data like images, audio, or text. Heavy Post-Processing: Human experts must spend time analyzing the clusters to understand what they mean.
Automatic Feature Extraction: Reduces data complexity on its own by automatically finding the most important traits. High Algorithmic Complexity: Calculating similarities across massive datasets requires intense computing power.
