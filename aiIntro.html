What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to imitate human intelligence. It allows computers to learn from data, recognize patterns, solve problems, and make decisions.

History of AI

1950s

The Beginning

The journey of AI began when researchers explored creating machines that could simulate human intelligence. Alan Turing introduced the Turing Test in 1950.

1960s-1980s

Growth and Development

AI expanded through expert systems and improved computing power. Machine learning techniques allowed computers to analyze data.

2010s

Resurgence of AI

Deep learning and big data caused rapid AI growth. Image recognition and voice assistants became popular.

2020s

Generative AI Era

Generative AI allows machines to create text, images and videos, transforming technology.

Why AI Exists?

Artificial Intelligence was created to help humans handle complex tasks, analyze large amounts of information, and improve efficiency. By allowing machines to learn and make decisions, AI can assist people in solving problems, automating repetitive work, and creating new possibilities across different industries.
