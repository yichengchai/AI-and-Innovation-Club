What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

AI is transforming the way of people work, communicate, and solve problems. As the usage of AI become more widely, AI is becoming an essential part of modern life.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning is one of the most poweful branches of AI. Instead of being explicitly programmed, computers learn the patterns from the data and improve their performance through experience.

Prompt engineering workshops

Prompt Engineering is the skill of communicating effectively with AI models such as ChatGPT and Gemini. A well-designed prompt helps AI generate more accurate and useful response.
